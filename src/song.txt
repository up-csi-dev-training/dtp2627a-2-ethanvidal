About the song:
"Kiss on My List," originally recorded by Hall & Oates in 1980, was covered by the indie pop duo The Bird and the Bee (Inara George and Greg Kurstin) for their 2010 tribute album, Interpreting the Masters Volume 1: A Tribute to Daryl Hall and John Oates. The track reimagines the upbeat, synth-driven '80s classic as a sleek, bossa-nova-infused indie pop track, replacing the polished rock bounce of the original with breezy vocals, lush retro synthesizer textures, and understated percussion. Narratively, the song explores the subtle irony of a lover insisting that a partner's kiss—while special—is merely one item among many on a list of things that make them happy, blending lighthearted romantic affection with a playful, pragmatic detachment.
